About NEXUS

NEXUS is a recruitment intelligence middleware that crawls 100+ public sources, scores discovered jobs using ATS matching (skills, role, location, batch, comp, company), trust analysis (payment detection, domain age, company footprint), freshness scoring (deadline-based decay), and niche detection.

Built with FastAPI, asyncpg, Playwright, and Celery. Deployed on Hugging Face Spaces.
